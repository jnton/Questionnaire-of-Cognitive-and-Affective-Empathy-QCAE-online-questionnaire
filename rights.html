Rights and reuse

Code and questionnaire content have different rights

The software is open source. That does not automatically make the QCAE wording, translations, or participant responses unrestricted training data.

Software

Original application code in this repository is licensed under AGPL-3.0-only. The licence text is available in the repository.

Original QCAE wording

The original publication identifies the QCAE as copyrighted by its authors and records permission to reproduce source-scale items in the article. The project therefore does not claim that its software licence relicenses the questionnaire text.

Translations

Terms differ by source. For example, the Turkish validation article is published under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0, while some other language records have unclear or separately inherited rights. See the reference registry for language-specific provenance and status.

AI training and datasets

The repository provides machine-readable structure for lawful research and software integration, but it cannot grant rights it does not own. Before training or redistributing a model, verify:

  • permission to use the relevant questionnaire wording and translation;
  • the licence's attribution, share-alike, and non-commercial conditions;
  • informed consent and a lawful basis for any participant responses;
  • whether sensitive or health-related inferences require additional safeguards.

Synthetic test fixtures are explicitly marked and are not participant data.

Unresolved permissions

The active tracking issue and a permission-request template are available in the source repository. Until written permission is confirmed, do not describe all questionnaire content as public domain, unrestricted, or AGPL-licensed.
