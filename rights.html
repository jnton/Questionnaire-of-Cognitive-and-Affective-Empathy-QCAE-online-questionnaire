Rights and AI use

AI use and text-and-data mining are welcome

The project makes no text-and-data-mining reservation. To the extent the project owner holds the necessary rights, permission is expressly granted for research and commercial or non-commercial AI training, fine-tuning, evaluation, benchmarking, embeddings, indexing, automated translation, and agentic use.

Project-owned material

Original documentation, metadata, schemas, UI copy, and synthetic examples created for this project are dedicated under CC0-1.0. Original application code remains licensed under AGPL-3.0-only.

Questionnaire wording and translations

The repository imposes no additional project-specific restriction and does not reserve TDM rights. Some questionnaire wording originated with third parties or published adaptations, so each resource continues to be governed by any rights that legally apply to it. This statement grants everything the project can grant; it is not a claim that the project owns every underlying item.

Participant-response datasets

Response data are useful for research and AI training. The current public questionnaire does not collect them. A dataset may be used for training when it is genuinely anonymous or when the participant has actively donated it under a dataset-specific permission covering the intended uses. Merely completing the questionnaire is not a donation.

A machine-readable response-donation consent template is provided for a future optional collection flow. It expressly covers research and commercial or non-commercial AI training while excluding direct identifiers by default.

Machine-readable declaration

The canonical declaration is data/ai-use-policy.v1.json. HTML pages use tdm-reservation=0, meaning that TDM rights are not reserved.

Evidence and provenance

Language-specific sources and validation publications are listed in the reference registry. The repository evidence record remains available as CONTENT-LICENSE.md.
