# Questionnaire content rights

## Software

The original application code in this repository is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**. See `LICENSE`.

## QCAE item wording

The software licence does **not** grant rights to the questionnaire text.

The original publication states that:

- the QCAE was assembled from items originating in the IRI, IVE, EQ, and HES;
- permission to use those source items was obtained by the QCAE authors;
- the QCAE is copyrighted by its authors; and
- Taylor & Francis received permission to reproduce the item content in the article.

Primary citation:

Reniers, R. L. E. P., Corcoran, R., Drake, R., Shryane, N. M., & Völlm, B. A. (2011). *The QCAE: A Questionnaire of Cognitive and Affective Empathy*. Journal of Personality Assessment, 93(1), 84–95. https://doi.org/10.1080/00223891.2010.528484

No repository-wide open-content permission from the QCAE rights holders has been independently confirmed. Public availability elsewhere is not evidence of an open licence.

## Translations

Translation source metadata is recorded in `data/qcae.v1.json`.

- French: sourced from a Frontiers article/supplement published under CC BY 4.0. This does not necessarily extinguish underlying rights in the original QCAE items.
- Portuguese (Portugal): sourced from a PLOS ONE article/supplement published under CC BY 4.0. The validated version excludes item 17.
- Serbian (Latin): source metadata reports CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; this should be checked directly against the OSF record before redistribution outside this project.
- German: provenance is a public research-material repository, but the content licence and relationship to the later 2024 validated German version have not been confirmed.

## Required follow-up

For unambiguous public redistribution rights, obtain written permission from the QCAE rights holders covering:

1. online administration;
2. display and distribution of all 31 items;
3. machine-readable publication;
4. translations;
5. open-source repository inclusion; and
6. non-commercial and commercial reuse conditions.

Until then, do not describe the questionnaire content as open source or AGPL-licensed.
