# Questionnaire content rights

## Software

Original application code is licensed under **AGPL-3.0-only**. The software licence does not grant rights to third-party questionnaire wording, translations, publications, or participant data.

## Original QCAE

The original publication states that the QCAE was assembled from items originating in other instruments, that permissions were obtained for those source items, and that the QCAE is copyrighted by its authors. No repository-wide unrestricted content licence has been independently confirmed.

Primary source: Reniers et al. (2011), DOI `10.1080/00223891.2010.528484`.

## Language sources

The canonical registry is `data/references.v1.json`.

- **Portuguese (Portugal):** validation article and supplement published by PLOS under CC BY; DOI `10.1371/journal.pone.0197755`. Underlying QCAE rights may still apply.

- **Turkish:** validation article is CC BY-NC-SA 3.0; DOI `10.29399/npa.27248`, with correction DOI `10.29399/npa.28743`. Underlying QCAE rights may still apply.

## AI training and participant data

Machine-readable publication is a technical feature, not a blanket permission grant. Training or redistributing models with questionnaire text requires compliance with the relevant content licences and inherited rights. Training with participant responses additionally requires informed consent, a lawful data-protection basis, purpose limitation, security, and any necessary research ethics approval.

The repository contains only explicitly marked synthetic examples, not a participant dataset.

## Required follow-up

Obtain or locate written permissions covering online administration, source-repository publication, machine-readable distribution, translation use, and commercial/non-commercial conditions. Resolve the open rights issue before claiming unrestricted reuse.
